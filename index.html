This is a plain HTML website, like most custom sites. It shows how HRL AI blogs appear on any website. Open the Blog page to see blogs published from HRL AI.

How the blog got here

We added this site in HRL AI as “Any website”, downloaded the ready blog.html file, and uploaded it next to this home page. That is the only change made to the website. Every blog published in HRL AI now appears on the Blog page by itself.
